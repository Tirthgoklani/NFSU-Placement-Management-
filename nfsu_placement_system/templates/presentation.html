Context & Vulnerabilities

The Campus Recruitment Challenge: Manual Chaos & Security Gaps

Campus placements handle high-stakes financial data, personal student identities, and strict institutional rules. Manual spreadsheets and generic portals introduce critical failure modes:

Policy Violations & Unfair Hoarding

Students placed in high-package firms attempt unauthorized switches, or take multiple internship-only roles, depriving peers of opportunities. Manual verifications miss the 1.5x CTC requirement for Category B.

Confidentiality & Privacy Breaches

Spreadsheets shared on messaging channels expose student personal mobile numbers, emails, and sensitive salary details without access control or masking, violating basic privacy standards.

Unregulated Absenteeism & Disputes

Candidates register for recruitment drives and skip pre-placement talks (PPTs) or interviews without excuse. Enforcing the policy-mandated 3-drive debarment is prone to manual bias and loss of tracking.

Inflated & Distorted Placement Metrics

Generic systems often conflate monthly internship stipends (e.g., ₹50k/mo) with annual full-time CTCs (LPA), or double-count candidates holding both, presenting misleading NIRF/NAAC data.

Our Solution Objective: A zero-trust, rule-enforced, forensically audited platform tailored specifically for NFSU's 2026–27 guidelines.

Innovation 2

Forensic Cybersecurity & Zero-Trust Defense

Designed for a premier National Forensic Sciences University with defense-in-depth measures against common and targeted cyber attacks:

Field-Level AES-256-GCM AEAD Encryption

PII fields (mobile numbers, emails, stipends) are encrypted at rest using AES-256 authenticated encryption. Even with direct physical database access, sensitive personal numbers cannot be deciphered.

Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Blockchain Ledger

Every administrative change, offer entry, and student update is cryptographically chained to the previous record's SHA-256 hash and signed with HMAC-SHA256. Any database record tampering is instantly detected.

IDOR & Granular RBAC Verification

Tested against Insecure Direct Object Reference (IDOR). Students attempting to inspect or alter peers' profiles are blocked with HTTP 403 Forbidden and immediate security incident logging.

RFC 6238 TOTP Multi-Factor Authentication

Administrators and officers support hardware/app TOTP (Google Authenticator / Authy), combined with brute-force rate-limiting that locks IPs after 5 failed attempts.

Security Hardening: HTTP Strict Headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options), Parameterized SQL, No eval/unsafe scripts.
